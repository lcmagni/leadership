This assessment is built on the theoretical framework of Generative Leadership of Meanings (Magni, Marchetti & Alharbi · Luiss Business School Open Research Books). The book argues that leadership is fundamentally an act of meaning-making — and that the most generative leaders simultaneously drive operational execution, invest in people, and actively shape the semantic landscape of their organisations.

The GLOM model identifies eight leadership styles produced by the intersection of three dimensions: orientation toward Tasks, People, and Meanings. Each style has distinct strengths, risks, and a developmental trajectory — and each is more or less suited to the organisational situation in which it operates.
